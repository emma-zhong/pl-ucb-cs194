Top customers by transaction count

A company has three stores: Store A, Store B, and Store C. Every year, the company identifies the 10 customers with the highest total count of transactions combined across all stores in that year. It then computes the total dollar amount those 10 customers spent at each store during the year in which they were selected.

The source DataFrame is named raw_df. Each row represents one transaction and has these columns:

ColumnDescription
transaction_ida unique identifier for the transaction
customer_idthe customer associated with the transaction
store_idthe store where the transaction occurred
yearthe year of the transaction
amountthe dollar amount of the transaction

A scatterplot will use year on the x-axis, the total amount spent by that year's selected customers on the y-axis, and store_id for the point shape. The table used for the plot must therefore contain exactly one row for each year and store.

Write an analysis plan that transforms raw_df into the table needed for the plot. Saving the plan generates editable pandas code.

  • The same customer may be selected in more than one year.
  • You may assume there are at least 10 unique customers in every year.
  • Your final table must contain only rows needed for the plot. Extra columns are allowed.
  • You may create and name intermediate results so later steps can refer to them.

Describe every operation clearly in English. Supported operation phrases include:

  • use table, select, and group rows by
  • compute count of and compute sum of
  • order by with ascending or descending
  • keep the first
  • join with the table names and matching columns

Name every table and column exactly as shown. Include the grouping columns, calculation inputs, sort direction, row count, and join keys needed by your steps. Code-like shorthand such as groupby or df[...] is not accepted as English analysis-plan wording.
